# WanderNav Privacy Policy
Last updated: 23 October 2026

**What WanderNav collects**
 - **Location.** WanderNav uses your location when you record a route, center the map on yourself, or save a pin. Routes and pins are stored on your device.
 - **Route matching.** To match a route to roads, its coordinates are sent to WanderNav's server. The server uses them only to compute the match and returns the result. Coordinates are not stored or logged on the server.
 - **Anonymous usage analytics.** WanderNav uses PostHog to collect anonymous usage events, such as when a recording starts or finishes, whether matching succeeded, and app version and device type. These events don't include your location, road names or any coordinates. IP addresses are discarded. Events are tied to a random identifier created on your device, not your name, email or account.

**What WanderNav doesn't do**
- No accounts and no sign-in.
- Your data isn't sold or shared for advertising.

**Deleting your data**
Deleting the app removes all routes stored on your device. Anonymous analytics events aren't linked to your identity.
